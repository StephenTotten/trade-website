01 / THE PREMISE

A memory missing.
A truth waiting.

After a violent incident at school that he cannot remember, seventeen-year-old Jace Cooper is admitted to Greenbriar Mental Health Center for evaluation.

As his relationships begin to unravel, Jace discovers that both his blackouts and the institution may be hiding something far more disturbing than anyone realizes.
